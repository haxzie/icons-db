# Notice & attribution

This set contains **500 iOS app icons**, one for each of the most-rated apps on the
US App Store. Unlike every other collection on IconsDB, these icons are **not
openly licensed**.

## Rights

Each icon is the property of its respective app publisher. Icons are reproduced
here for identification purposes — to let people find and reference the app they
mean. No affiliation with, sponsorship by, or endorsement from any publisher is
implied, including Apple.

`manifest.json` records, for every icon: the publisher's legal name, the App Store
bundle id and numeric app id, and a direct link to the app's store listing. That is
enough to identify and contact the rights holder for any icon in the set.

Artwork originates from Apple's public iTunes lookup API. The `square` variant is
byte-equivalent to what Apple serves. The `rounded` variant applies the iOS icon
mask and is therefore a modified reproduction.

## Removal requests

Publishers who want their icon removed should contact <hello@iconsdb.app>. Removal
is honoured on request, without requiring a formal takedown notice, and does not
need the request to establish infringement. Expected turnaround is five working
days; removals are applied to the hosted set and to the next published version.

## For users of this set

Downloading an icon from here grants you no rights in the mark it depicts. Using a
company's icon to identify that company — a login button, an integration list, a
comparison table — is generally fine. Using it as your own product's icon, or in a
way that suggests the publisher endorses you, is not, and that exposure is yours
rather than ours.
